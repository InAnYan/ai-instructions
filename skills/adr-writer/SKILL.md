# ADR Writer Skill

Assists in creating and maintaining Architecture Decision Records (ADRs).

## Core Sections

- **Title & Numbering**: Sequential numbering and concise title (e.g., `ADR-001-use-sqlite-for-cache.md`).
- **Status**: Proposed, Accepted, Deprecated, or Superseded.
- **Context & Problem Statement**: The situation, constraints, and motivating factors.
- **Decision Drivers**: Factors prioritized (speed, maintainability, cost, etc.).
- **Considered Options**: Alternatives evaluated with pros and cons.
- **Decision Outcome**: Chosen approach and rationale.
- **Consequences**: Positive, negative, and neutral trade-offs.
