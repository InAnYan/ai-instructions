# Technology Research Skill

Guides the investigation and selection of libraries, frameworks, tools, or architectural patterns.

## Responsibilities

1. **Analyze Requirements**: Parse what problem needs solving, target runtime/language, and operational constraints.
2. **Discover Options**: Search current industry options, open-source projects, and emerging alternatives.
3. **Comparative Evaluation**:
   - Maintenance status, community health, documentation quality
   - License compatibility and security posture
   - Performance, footprint, and learning curve
4. **Recommendation Summary**: Provide clear, trade-off based recommendations to help the user choose.
